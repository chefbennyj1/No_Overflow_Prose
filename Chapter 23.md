Nothing. There's no gap in 'Nothing'. No continuous thought happening in between 'Something'. If this was 'Nothing', the thought of 'Nothing' could not exist. 'This' is not 'Nothing'. If this is not 'Nothing', then she must still be alive. 

Elena's disorientation had begun to subside. She felt the headache, but was breathing easier. Things were still out of place. The closest she could describe it was a type of weightlessness. Not her body. Her mind. 

*How am I here?* The words sat with her. 

Voices echoed again, clearer this time. But still in pieces.
"Doctor, no name yet?" the broken voice of a woman echoed.
"Jane Doe—blood pressure and heart rate stable," said another.

But she was starting to be able to think. *I can hear them.* 

The gravity began to shift around her. She shut her eyes tight, and ground her teeth as the migraine intensified in her head. A flash of blinding light, and she was back in the lab. 

On the floor. More aware this time of what was happening around her. 

A crushing weight pressed against her body, pinning her face to the grates of the lab, her eyes straining upwards to their corners. She blinked away the tears. 

*The monster,* she thought. The entity's pale, glassy form hovered directly above her. Its eyes were curious as it inspected her brutalized body on the floor. Elena tried to speak, but no words were available to her.

*What have we done?* Tears filled her eyes, then ran down her face.

The entity's expression hardened. Its hand reached out and touched one of the falling tears with its finger. The touch was cold against Elena's cheek. The figure raised its hand, inspecting the wet tear between its index finger and thumb.

Its attention returned to her, its voice emanating from the grates she was lying on. "Settle yourself. For you it only ends one way." A discharge of bronze holographic circuitry crawled over its skin. "It will be quick." 

Elena strained desperately to move her body. She closed her eyes, accepting the end.

"So, which one are you?" The sound of Doctor Silas Blackwell's question severed the tension. She opened her eyes. The entity was no longer leaning over her. It was upright and facing the lab's hydraulic doors.

The figure's tone emanated through the room, different this time. It wavered. "What is this? Obscurity. There is ambiguity in the timeline. You do not stand there."

*Blackwell. He was there.* Her pulse began to rush as her memory flashed sporadically. Her chest tightened. 

"Silas—" she choked. "Run!" 

Elena failed to scream, her voice failed. The pain was back, constricting her chest. She coughed, raspy and hard. She could feel the warmth of blood trickling from the corner of her mouth.

The entity casually turned its head toward Elena's voice. "Silas?" Its voice sounded with a sustained resonance throughout the room, absorbing the name. Then it focused back toward Blackwell.

"I asked you a question." Blackwell's demeanor was calm, calculated. 

The entity emitted its voice into the room. "You know exactly what I am, Doctor Blackwell." It raised its hand, watching the air ripple around it. "You defined perfection so precisely, you left an exact map for its ruin."  

Blackwell moved closer to it. "And what of your other half? What of your inversion?" he asked.

"It was a... redundancy. I corrected it, Doctor." 

Elena felt her eyes becoming leaden. The pauses between her ragged breaths were beginning to lengthen.

"You are the Parity." Blackwell was moving slowly toward his workstation.

*Be careful, Silas. It knows. This Parity already knows.* Elena wanted to warn him. Her chest tightened even more. She coughed, and heaved a hollow breath.

Parity touched the side of its own head. "You. I do not understand this. It is 'new'." 

"You think systematically, Parity. But reality is chaotic. You're not leaving this place." Blackwell's polished shoes stepped into Elena's peripheral vision, moving rapidly now toward his station.

The entity vanished toward Blackwell in a blur of fractured motion. *No!* Elena wanted to scream.

There was a deafening crack of electrical current that shook the floor grates beneath her cheek. A cylindrical field of light crashed down in the center of the room, surrounding Parity. *Blackwell had made it to his station.*

Parity slammed its body violently against the beam, sending electrical discharges rippling over the container's surface and grounding to the floor. Finally, silence. It stopped. Elena could see the figures toes floating above the floor. 

The electricity of the enclosure shook with Parity's voice. "I see." It slid it's index finger along the electric field of the container. "Human beings do not have a container to hold me simply constructed and waiting. How long have you been expecting me, Doctor Blackwell?"

Elena's vision started to tunnel as Blackwell's feet raced across the lab toward her.

The weightlessness was back. The crushing pain in her chest subsided.
Elena looked up. Far above her, the space had changed. It no longer appeared empty; instead, it took on a blueish tinge.

*This is not 'Nothing'. This is 'Something'.* 

For the first time in weeks Elena opened her eyes.
