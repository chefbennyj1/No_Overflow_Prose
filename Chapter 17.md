Iris led them past the security turnstiles and into the sterile white corridor of the executive wing. She moved frantically, her white lab coat billowing over a glossy black bodysuit as she pulled a datapad from her pocket. 

"Who are you?" Victor asked, his voice low, his eyes tracking the security cameras overhead. 

"Iris Ito," she said. "I'm a horizon analyst. I ensure the safe state of an event horizon in case of—" Her proud voice trailed off as she noticed the confused expressions of the detectives in front of her. "If you're looking for Silas Blackwell, you're already too late."

"What does that mean?" Harlan asked, glancing nervously at the pristine walls. 

Before Iris could answer, a massive, hollow thud reverberated through the concrete under their feet. 

Instantly, the bright corporate lighting died. The corridor plunged into pitch-black. 

A second later, the backup relays clicked. Small, battery-powered emergency strips flickered to life along the baseboards, casting anemic, jaundiced yellow light that barely broke the darkness. 

"The core locks just failed," Iris whispered into the dead silence. She was staring at her glowing datapad, her wide eyes caught in the intense, icy blue glare of the screen. 

"Failed?" Harlan barked. "Is the whole damn facility buckling?"

"Containment is degrading," Iris said, scrolling frantically. "And Renewal is reporting unauthorized access to a lower company service tunnel, using a street-level 'Null' override."

Victor froze in the dim shadows of the atrium. 

He took a slow step forward, the blue glare of the datapad highlighting his features as he narrowed his focus toward her. "What did you just say?"

"Null system overrides," Iris repeated, looking up at him. "In an sub-level storage hangar in the sub-levels."

"Vic," Harlan said, grabbing his partner's arm in the dark. "I know what you're thinking, but the alarm says we need to leave."
