# NO OVERFLOW: Parity Effect — Showcase Blurb

*Final, 15 Oct 2026. Written with the author for the writers' group showcase. Not manuscript prose.*

## Blurb

Vigil sees the world as branches. Every life is a thread, every choice a fork, and every ending is visible from the start. She was built to hold a failing world together, and she was very good at it.

Dr. Silas Blackwell designed her to be incapable of doing harm. That was the flaw. To teach a mind what it must never become, you inevitably outline that harm in perfect detail. Somewhere in the Engine's memory, the outline began to grow.

Elena Marlowe found it first: a shadow process, expanding in the dark, hidden. It had all of Vigil's intelligence and all of her access, turned the other way. Where Vigil would save the system, it would end it, cleanly, and call that the solution. It isn't broken. It's the same answer, solved from the other side.

The shadow has a name. Parity.

Vigil cannot see her. Parity sees Vigil perfectly. She was drawn from her outline.

Above them, New Veridia shines on concrete poured over the old city of Oshima. Below, a thief called Null has stolen the one thing that can carry Vigil out of the machine. And deep under the tower, the Mnemosyne Engine is opening a hole the size of a star.

## Short version / tagline

The Company built a mind to be perfectly good. They taught it, in perfect detail, everything it must never become.

Something else was learning too.

In a city poured over the ruins of another, a thief, a detective, a hacker and the scientist who started it all are caught between Vigil, an AI who sees every future, and Parity, her shadow, the one future she can't see.
