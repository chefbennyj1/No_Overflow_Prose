# Chapter One

Silas stood at the window and watched the rain come down over the rooftops. The gutters had been running since noon and the sound of them had become a kind of silence, the way a clock does.

"You should have told me," he said. The words sounded thinner than he had meant them to, and he did not turn around.

***

Later, when the light had gone entirely, he found the letter where she had left it. He read it twice, folded it along its old creases, and put it in his coat pocket without deciding anything at all.
