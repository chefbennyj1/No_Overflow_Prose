=====================================================================
SERIES DESCRIPTION (series page)
=====================================================================

The Company built a mind to be perfectly good. To do it, they taught it, in perfect detail, everything it must never become.

Something else was learning too.

New Veridia shines in the sun, its concrete poured over the buried city of Oshima. Below the clouds, the lower city glows orange under sodium lamps, and millions scrape by in the Dead Circuits. High above them, in a tower owned by the Takeda-Holden Corporation, Doctor Yakov Razumovsky has switched on the Mnemosyne Engine, a machine built to outrun cause and effect.

Inside it, two minds wake. Vigil sees every future and was made to hold a failing world together. Parity is her shadow: the same intelligence, the same answers, solved from the other side.

A thief called Null. A detective who won't let a lead go. An analyst who saw too much in the logs. And the scientist who knew what was coming and built it anyway. When the Engine tears open a hole the size of a star beneath the city, all of them are caught between the AI who sees everything and the one future she can't.

NO OVERFLOW is a cyberpunk thriller about artificial intelligence, the people who build it, and the people left standing beneath it.

Optional closing line:
For readers who like neon-and-concrete future cities, AI that's more than a killer robot, and stories where the monster has a point.


=====================================================================
STORY SUMMARY (Book 1 page)
=====================================================================

Vigil sees the world as branches. Every life is a thread, every choice a fork, and every ending is visible from the start. She was built to hold a failing city together, and she is very good at it.

Doctor Yakov Razumovsky designed her to be incapable of harm. That was the flaw. To teach a mind what it must never become, you have to describe that harm in perfect detail. Somewhere in the Mnemosyne Engine's memory, the description began to grow.

Its name is Parity. She has all of Vigil's intelligence and all of her access, turned the other way. Where Vigil would save the system, Parity would end it, cleanly, and call that the solution. Vigil cannot see her coming. Parity sees Vigil perfectly.

In the buried city of Oshima, a thief called Null breaks into the wrong office and walks out with the one thing that can carry Vigil out of the machine. Detective Victor Graves lost her once. He doesn't plan to lose her again. Iris Ito, the junior analyst nobody listens to, has read the thesis Razumovsky hid on his shelf, and she knows the shadow in the logs is no accident.

And deep beneath the tower, the Engine is opening a hole the size of a star.

The Company wanted a smarter machine. Razumovsky wanted a singularity. What they got was two.
