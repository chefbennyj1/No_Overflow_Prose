She looked down at the SBC in her hand. The string of text Vigil had given her stabilized, resolving into a specific serial number on the small green screen: **#77-B**.

Harper looked down the endless, vanishing rows of plastic-draped bodies. "Rin... there are hundreds of them. It's going to take us hours to find one specific serial number in the dark."

Before Rin could answer, a dull mechanical thunk echoed from the walls. 

Beneath their feet, a single square of the white paneled floor illuminated, diffusing a cold, permeating light. Then the panel in front of it lit up. Then the next. 

A glowing line traced its way across the floor, cutting a flawless path through the dormant army, until it stopped—illuminating the floor directly beneath a single synthetic in the dead center of the room.
