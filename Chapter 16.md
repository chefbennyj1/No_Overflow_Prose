She looked down at the SBC in her hand. The string of text Vigil had given her stabilized, resolving into a specific serial number on the small green screen: **#77-B**.

Harper looked down the endless, vanishing rows of plastic-draped bodies. "Rin... there are hundreds of them. It's going to take us hours to find one specific serial number."

Before Rin could answer, a dull mechanical thunk echoed from the walls. 

Deep within the grid, a single square of the white paneled floor shifted color, illuminating with a white light. It sat like a isolated beacon, shining directly beneath a single dormant synthetic. 

Rin and Harper navigated the grid, stopping at the illuminated panel. They synthetic the loose plastic sheet draped over it's vinyl face. 

Rin dropped to her knees, inspecting the floor panel beneath the dormant body. She found a  small access panel flush with the floor tile. Pressing it down, it popped up revealing a recessed array of industrial ports lined the base. She found a braided cable coiled inside. She pulled the connector free, and inspected the end. The metal housing was stamped: **NET-CONN**. 

Rin went o jam the cord into the network port on the synth, Harper reached out and grabbed her wrist. "Hey, you sure about this?" She surveilled the area, before making eye contact with Rin.

Rin jammed the heavy cord into the network port and executed the handshake. 

For a long moment in the hangar nothing. 

Then, the peristaltic pump beside them seized. The silicone tubes feeding into the back of the synthetic suddenly snapped. Rin cursed and Harper moved back as they tubes fell to the ground spilling translucent blue fluid into a puddle on the white tile.  

A sharp, mechanical gasp ripped through the silence as the body took a simulated breath. 

Behind the loose, transparent plastic sheet, the synthetic's eyes snapped open. They were not the spinning rings of synthetic iris, instead they burned with a blinding, incandescent white light, edged with flares of  blue that pierced straight through the plastic. 

As Vigil turned her head to look at Rin and Harper, the piercing beams of her eyes swept across the grid. The harsh light caught the hundreds of dormant bodies, casting long, stretching shadows against the white walls that made the entire sleeping army look as if it were writhing in the dark.
