"Four weeks, Rin," Detective Victors Graves sounded too much like an asshole, his voice trailed off. The orange, one piece jumpsuit Rin wore was a size to big. It hung loose around the white tank under it. A serial number stenciled on the breast and across the back, **D.O.C. 398**. 

"How's the food?" A sly grin across his face.

This wasn't the first time in the last month she found herself sitting at the stainless steel table, her wrists cuffed to the center. The room's dirty white square tiling hadn't been cleaned, ever. But, neither had the cell and the cot she had been sleep on.

A security camera recorded their interaction from the upper corner of the room. A large rectangular mirror spanned the length of the far wall of the interrogation room.

Victor Graves dropped a manila digital folder on the table in front of her. He placed his index finger on it and leaned in, staring down at her across the table. "That file keeps getting bigger." 

Rin didn't speak. She knew better that to say anything, she looked up out of the top of her eyes.
