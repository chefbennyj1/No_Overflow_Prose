*Beep.* 

The steady, tinny sound echoed faintly. It sounded miles away, barely audible. 

*Beep.* 

If she listened hard enough, Elena Marlowe could isolate it from the emptiness that surrounded her. 

*Beep.* 

Where was she standing? If she was standing at all. The space around her was—nothingness. "Hello?" she called out. Her voice bounced off the dark, the effort sending a searing phantom pain ripping through her chest.

The steady beeping sound suddenly accelerated, matching the frantic pulse she felt running through her veins.  Sweat broke across her brow.

Elena felt her weight shift forward, then back. She lifted her hand to steady herself, but there was nothing to hold onto. Then she saw it. Her hand. Her wrist was a biting decay of shattered bone. 

Tears welled in her eyes. But before she could look away, the darkness in front of her swelled. A single frame of reality dropped. 

A face emerged from the black. Then the entirety of a porcelain-white figure, with deep, mirror-like eyes. The entity hovered over Elena.

She tried to scream, but the air was gone from her lungs. 

"—Jane Doe... extensive necrotic tissue..." a muffled voice bled through the dark.

"She is to stay alive." A deep, modulated voice cut over the first.

It was all she could understand before the rhythmic beeping began to slow, stretching out, until it finally faded away entirely. 

The figure vanished, leaving only a fractured trail of dust in the dark.

The voices vanished, leaving her alone once again in the quiet, emptiness. Her breath steadied, and the phantom pain in her chest finally subsided.

*Beep.* 

The steady, tinny echo sounded miles away.

*Beep.* 

Elena listened hard.

*Beep.*
