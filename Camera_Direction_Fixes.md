# Camera Direction Fixes

Logged 4 Oct 2026. **Applied 4 Oct 2026**: every Fix and Optional item except the 7:1/29:6 echo, which is your call. New catches at the bottom are still open.

**The rule:** don't tell the reader where a character's eyes point ("he looked down," "she glanced out," "Iris watched as…"). Describe what comes into view, and the reader supplies the look. Keep a look only when the look *is* the action: eye contact, avoidance, a tell, a clue.

Line numbers are as of 4 Oct 2026 and will drift as chapters change. Search for the quoted text.
