# The Heartbreak Promise

Source: Plot Luck (Brian), "The Writing Trick That Makes Every Sad Scene Hit Before It Even Happens" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OCXQHcLKUCI

A five-part framework for earned emotional devastation. Don't rely on a big, forced sad scene. Build the heartbreak across the whole arc so the loss is already waiting when it arrives.

1. **The Handhold** (2:08): Private, intimate moments and inside jokes between characters that make the bond real and tangible.
2. **The Future** (4:05): A shared dream. When it's taken away, the audience loses both the character and the future they were building.
3. **The Fault Line** (6:48): A subtle sense of dread, a "hairline fracture" planted early, signaling that the current happiness might not last.
4. **The Break** (8:52): The moment of loss. Use restraint. Trust the setup and let the audience's existing grief do the work instead of forcing drama.
5. **The Echo** (11:23): The aftermath. Show how the character's life is permanently altered (the "empty chair") so the story resonates after it ends.
