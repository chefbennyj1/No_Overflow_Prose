The dark of the observation deck was suffocating. Bronze holographic circuit traces seared across the entity's weightless frame, illuminating the concrete under it. Its hollow eyes, kept their inhuman gaze locked on Elena. 

Before Elena could move, the lab's overhead intercom crackled to life. Static cut through, followed by Silas Blackwell's voice. Stripped of its usual arrogance. Breathless, frantic, he was speaking into a microphone from somewhere deep in the stairwell. 

"Elena! It wasn't supposed to break through the boundary! It was supposed to be contained!" His voice echoed through the lab. "Run!"

Elena was already backing away, her eyes locked on the porcelain-white figure in front of her. She edged toward the red manual lockdown lever on the far wall. She would be trapped with the entity, but it couldn't be allowed to leave. 

Its head tilted, the movements stuttering into fractured trails of motion. It tracked Elena's movement toward the lever. 

"In every version of your existence where you move to pull that switch." The entity's voice vibrated through the elements in the room, a sound devoid of breath or origin. "I am already standing at it's end."

The air in the lab rippled. A single frame of reality dropped. In one millisecond, it was intimately close to Elena. The physical space between them only freezing molecules of air. 
The entity reached out and caught Elena’s wrist right as her fingertips brushed the red steel of the lever. Its necrotic touch felt like burning as the skin of Elena's wrist instantly turned a biting black. With an effortless, sickening twist, there was a sharp crack—the sound of a wet tree branch snapping in half. 

Elena felt the searing pain shoot up her arm as its open palm struck her chest. Her ribs fractured instantly. The air was violently evacuated from her lungs as she was launched backward into the dark. She flew across the width of the lab, before crashing brutally into the base of the reinforced glass. 

Elena crumpled to the concrete floor. No breath. No movement. Her vision began to narrow into a dark tunnel. 

*I Failed.*
