Nothing. There's no gap in 'Nothing'. No continuous thought happening in between 'Somethings'. If Elena was experiencing 'Nothing', she would perceive a seamless transition of being in the lab to whatever experience she would have next. This experience. Therefore 'This' is not 'Nothing'. If this is not 'Nothing', then she must still be alive. 

Elena's disorientation had begun to subside. She still felt a throbbing headache, but she was breathing easier. She still felt out of place. The closest she could describe it was a type of weightlessness. Not her body. Her mind.

*How am I here?* Her words sat with her. 

Voices echoed again, clearer this time. But, still in pieces.
"Doctor, no name yet?" the broken voice of a woman echoed.
"Jane Doe—blood pressure and heart rate stable—been weeks," said another.

But, she was starting to be able to think. Think about what she felt.
*They think I can't hear them.* 

Elena looked up. Far above her, the space had changed. It no longer appeared empty. Instead it took on a blueish tinge.

Suddenly, she felt gravity begin to shift around her. A weight she hadn't experienced. She squinted her eyes and ground her teeth as the migraine intensified in her head. A flash of blinding light, and she was back in the lab. 

On the floor unable to move, but not the excruciating pain she had felt before. She was more aware this time. Attentive to everything happening around her in the lab. 

A crushing gravity pressed against her body, holding her in place.

Her face pinned to the grates of the lab floor, her eyes straining to their corners. Upwards. She blinked away the blurring tears to see her view. 

*The monster,* she thought. The entity's pale, glassy form hovering directly above her. Its eyes were curious as it inspected her brutalized body on the floor. Elena tried to speak, but no words were available to her.

*What did we do?* Tears filled her eyes, overflowed, then ran down her face.

The entity's expression hardened, its hand reached out and touched one of the falling tears with its finger. The touch was cold against Elena's cheek. It raised its hand in front of its face, inspecting the wet tear between its index finger and thumb.

Its attention returned to her, the voice emanating from the grates she was lying on. "Settle yourself. For you it only ends one way. It will be quick. This, I will..." there was a pause as the entity looked up in thought, a discharge of bronze holographic circuitry crawled over its skin. It looked back at Elena as she strained desperately to move her body. "...promise you." Its voice sounded.
Elena closed her eyes, accepting the end.

"So, which one are you?" The sound of Doctor Silas Blackwell's question severed the tension. Elena felt a abrupt pause, she opened her eyes. The entity was no longer leaning over her. It was upright and facing the lab's hydraulic doors.

The tone emanated in the room wavered. "What is this? Obscurity. There is ambiguity in the timeline. You do not stand there."

*Blackwell? Was he there?*  Her pulse began to rush as her memory flashed sporadically.

Her chest tightened. It was Blackwell's voice again, "I asked you a question!"

*Silas, what are you doing? Run!*  She failed to scream. The pain was back, constricting her chest.  She coughed raspy and hard. She could feel warmth of blood trickling from the corner of her mouth.

"I asked you a question." Blackwell's demeanor was calm, calculated. 

The entity emitted it's voice into the room, "You know exact what I am. You defined perfection so precisely, you left an exact map for its ruin."  

Elena watched Blackwell move close to it. *He doesn't seem surprised by the monster.*  

"And, what of your other half. What of your inversion?" He asked.

"It was a... redundancy. I corrected it." He had it communicating.
Elena felt her eyes becoming heavy. The pauses between her heavy breaths were beginning to lengthen.

"You are the Parity?" Blackwell knew it's name. Elena's mind raced. She saw him moving toward the secondary lockdown switch. *It will know, Silas* Elena wanted to warn him. *This Parity already knows...* Her chest tightened even more. She coughed, and heaved a heavy breath.
