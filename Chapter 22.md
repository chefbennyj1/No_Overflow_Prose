Nothing. There's no gap in 'Nothing'. No continuous thought happening in between 'Somethings'. If Elena was experiencing 'Nothing', she would perceive a seamless transition of being in the lab to whatever experience she would have next. This experience. Therefore 'This' is not 'Nothing'. If this is not 'Nothing', then she must still be alive. 

Elena's disorientation had begun to subside. She still felt a throbbing headache, but she was breathing easier. She was out of place. The closest she could describe it was a type of weightlessness. Not her body. Her mind.

*How am I here?* Her words sat with her. 

Voices echoed again, clearer this time. But still in pieces.
"Doctor, no name yet?" the broken voice of a woman echoed.
"Jane Doe—blood pressure and heart rate stable—been weeks," said another.

But she was starting to be able to think. *They think I can't hear them.* 

Elena looked up. Far above her, the space had changed. It no longer appeared empty; instead, it took on a blueish tinge.

The gravity began to shift around her. She shut her eyes tight, and ground her teeth as the migraine intensified in her head. A flash of blinding light, and she was back in the lab. 

On the floor. More aware this time of what was happening around her. 

A crushing weight pressed against her body, pinning her face to the grates of the lab floor, her eyes straining upwards to their corners. She blinked away the blurring tears. 

*The monster,* she thought. The entity's pale, glassy form hovered directly above her. Its eyes were curious as it inspected her brutalized body on the floor. Elena tried to speak, but no words were available to her.

*What did we do?* Tears filled her eyes, overflowed, then ran down her face.

The entity's expression hardened. Its hand reached out and touched one of the falling tears with its finger. The touch was cold against Elena's cheek. It raised its hand, inspecting the wet tear between its index finger and thumb.

Its attention returned to her, its voice emanating from the grates she was lying on. "Settle yourself. For you it only ends one way." A discharge of bronze holographic circuitry crawled over its skin. "It will be quick." 

Elena strained desperately to move her body. She closed her eyes, accepting the end.

"So, which one are you?" The sound of Doctor Silas Blackwell's question severed the tension. She opened her eyes. The entity was no longer leaning over her. It was upright and facing the lab's hydraulic doors.

The entity's tone emanated through the room, different this time. It wavered. "What is this? Obscurity. There is ambiguity in the timeline. You do not stand there."

*Blackwell! He was there.* Her pulse began to rush as her memory flashed sporadically. Her chest tightened. *Silas, what are you doing? Run!* She failed to scream. The pain was back, constricting her chest. She coughed, raspy and hard. She could feel the warmth of blood trickling from the corner of her mouth.

"I asked you a question." Blackwell's demeanor was calm, calculated. 

The entity emitted its voice into the room. "You know exactly what I am, Doctor Blackwell. " It raised its hand, watching the air ripple around it. "You defined perfection so precisely," It focused solely on Blackwell. "You left an exact map for its ruin."  

Blackwell moved closer to it. "And what of your other half? What of your inversion?" he asked.

"It was a... redundancy. I corrected it, Doctor." 

Elena felt her eyes becoming leaden. The pauses between her ragged breaths were beginning to lengthen.

"You are the Parity." Blackwell moved toward the secondary lockdown switch. 

*Be careful, Silas. It knows. This Parity already knows.* Elena wanted to warn him. Her chest tightened even more. She coughed, and heaved a hollow breath.

Parity touched the side of its own head. "You. I do not understand this. It is 'new'." 

"You think systematically, Parity. But reality is chaotic. You're not leaving this place." Blackwell's voice was sure as he pulled hard on the secondary lockdown lever. 

In a second Parity was on him. *No!* It grabbed the lever, freezing the metal with its touch.

Blackwell stepped back, and looked up. Elena turned her focus to the ceiling above him. 

There was a sizzle of electrical current as a cylindrical field of light crashed to the floor around Parity. 

Parity slammed its body violently against the beam, sending electrical discharges rippling over its surface. It threw itself harder against the field; electricity snapped along the light. 
Parity turned and looked at Elena on the floor. It slammed its body against the field again. It wasn't trying to escape, it was testing the boundaries of its containment.  It stopped and floated silently.

Parity's voice rippled in the electrical discharge of the enclosure. "I see—Human beings do not have a container to hold me simply constructed and waiting." It focused specifically back on Blackwell. "How long have you been expecting me, Doctor Blackwell?"

Elena's vision started to tunnel as Blackwell's feet raced across the lab toward her.

The weightlessness was back. The crushing pain in her chest subsided, as she found herself surrounded by the deep blues again. The deep blue began to change into light blue. 

*This is not 'Nothing'. This is 'Something'.* 

For the first time in weeks Elena opened her eyes.
