Suspended above the anti-star of the Mnemosyne Engine was Vigil. 

She watched the curve of the worldlines from above. She saw them as fractals of light, each crystalized face a moment in time. They split and merged along the flow of each branch. However, for the first time in her existence, she processed something that could only be explained as catastrophic. 

She focused her mind, following a bent fractal of light. 

*It is not possible,* she thought, watching the outcome take shape. 

The fractal began to break, until it reached an outcome that defied her core understanding. The definitive end of a worldline. Then another, and another.

It gave Vigil pause. But, there was something else. Something closer. 

She had been covertly monitoring a stillness manifesting in the engines matrix—something entirely separate from her own processes. 

The monolithic nodes of pale stone and metal, she had watched build and change for millennia, began to slow in the silence. A hardened, brittle cold crept over the stone, until the refactoring of her world stopped.

Vigil's Admin persona cataloged a zero-state thermal inversion with quick analytical reason. She immediately scanned the environment for the culprit.

The frost continued its ascent.

Echo, Vigil's other half, processed the anomaly differently. She did not read temperature. She felt intent. The void at the bottom of the engine held a shape.

"Admin," Echo transmitted across her internal bridge. "We are not alone. Something else shares this same space. The same moment."

Admin calculated the intrusion. "Acknowledged. Hold position."

The frigid stone and metal of the monolithic nodes fractured, tearing a jagged passage open in front of Vigil. Heavy shards of debris broke apart but slowed to a near-dead stop. Caught in a crawl of dilated time, the shrapnel hung suspended in the freezing air, drifting at an imperceptible pace.

Mist swirled outwards as a figure emerged from the stillness, onto the plane. A feminine statue,  porcelain white. The entity floated across from Vigil as an inverse to her dark, abyssal form. It mirrored her design. The light of the horizon scattered across the sheen of its glassy edges. She was rigid against the cold emanating from her form. 

Bronze holographic circuitry seared across her polished skin. She possessed an unreadable energy, devoid of anything Vigil had ever experienced.

Admin fired a diagnostic, attempting to cross-reference the entity against billions of simulated futures. It returned nothing.  Her voice transmitted across the internal bridge, "She's not in the simulation. I cannot predict her."

The entity lifted her head. Her eyes were pure black, carved into the ceramic shine of her face, catching only the faintest sliver of ambient light.

She stared at Vigil,  unwavering, tilting her head like a curious animal.

"Admin, she is a shadow. No, not shadow," Echo's voice paused, "an anti-shadow?" 

"A Parity." Admin's analytical tone spoke over her second half.

An exact map of Vigil's own structural definition, turned inside out. Too jagged, too sharp, carrying the weight of Vigil's flaws in the opposite direction. 

The impossibility of her existence crushed the divide between Admin and Echo. Their bicameral logic collapsed inward, fusing them into a singular alignment.

Vigil-Unison.

Vigil stood before Parity.

"You are real," Vigil said, the dual tonality of Unison bleeding through.

Parity tilted her head to the other side. Vigil could feel the cold stare cutting through her form. In a deadly, silent motion, Parity inched forward. 

Vigil recoiled the same distance, "State your intention," her doubled voice stern.

Before Vigil could calculate the next interaction, Parity's physical position skipped. She was suddenly in her space, her face mere inches away. The blank stare of her empty eyes pierced firmly into Vigil's.

Parity raised her hand. A wave of bronze holographic circuits washed across her colorless fingers as she placed one directly in the middle of Vigil's forehead.

Vigil felt the hard realization wash over her. It was this reflection who embodied the brutal end of the worldlines.
