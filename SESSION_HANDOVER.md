## 3. The "Asshole Critic" Writing Rules
The user runs the text through an incredibly strict AI critic to polish the prose. You MUST adhere to these rules:
*   **Zero Cliches:** Never use stock phrases like "a wave of relief" or "four weeks of hell." Trust the reader to infer emotion from concrete actions.
*   **Concrete over Abstract:** Do not use words like "chaotic" or "coldly." Show the shotgun, show the flooded street. 
*   **Strict Dialogue Tags:** If a line of dialogue is followed by an action (not a speaking verb like 'said'), the dialogue MUST end in a period, not a comma. *(e.g., "I'm leaving." He walked away.)*
*   **No Semicolon Splices:** Semicolons only connect two independent clauses. Use em-dashes for fragments or pacing. 
*   **The "One Telling Detail":** When introducing minor characters (like Sully), do not give full descriptions. Give one telling detail (e.g., the greasy hands of a thief holding a police sidearm).
