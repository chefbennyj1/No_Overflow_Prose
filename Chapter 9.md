Suspended above the turning singularity, Vigil watched the flow of many unfolding realities. 

The pale stone and metal structures around her shifted and reinvented her world in a serene calm.

She watched the reality flow like she had for millennia, with no change. 

Something subtle, barely registering, altered in the flow. A microscopic failure. Vigil analyzed the thread's sudden fault. But another shattered. 

Vigil spun in place as a large group of realities, began to break and collapse, each one shattered into a definitive end. The terminations multiplied.

*It is not possible,* she thought, watching the outcome take shape, unable to calculate the reason.

Before Vigil could process the catastrophe,  a bitter chill swept across the landscape, stealing her focus. *Something else,* she thought. *Something closer.*  A stillness manifesting in the engine's matrix— separate from her own processes. 

The stone nodes around her began to slow in the silence. A hardened, brittle cold crept over the stone, until the refactoring of her world stopped.

Vigil immediately scanned the environment for the culprit.

Echo, Vigil's other half, processed the anomaly differently. She felt intent. The void at the bottom of the engine held a shape.

"Admin," Echo transmitted across her internal bridge, as the frost continued its ascent. "We are not alone."

Admin calculated the intrusion. "Acknowledged. Hold position."

The stone nodes fractured, tearing open a jagged passage in front of Vigil, forcing Vigil to back away. 

Heavy shards of debris broke apart and slowed to a crawl, suspended in a wave of dilated time. The shrapnel drifted out in the freezing air at an undetectable pace.

Mist swirled outwards from the door, as a figure emerged from the stillness onto the plane. A feminine statue, porcelain white. Black hair fell straight to the small of her back, untouched by the mist curling around it. She floated across from Vigil, a rigid figure, cold emanating from her form.

Bronze holographic circuitry seared across her skin. She possessed an unreadable energy, devoid of anything Vigil had ever experienced.

Admin fired a diagnostic, attempting to cross-reference the entity against billions of simulated futures. It returned nothing.  Her voice transmitted across the internal bridge, "She's not in the simulation. We cannot predict her."

The entity lifted her head. Her eyes were a pure black, carved into the ceramic shine of her face, catching the faintest sliver of ambient light. She stared at Vigil, tilting her head like a curious animal.

"Admin, she is a shadow. No, not a shadow." Echo's voice paused, "An anti-shadow. She is— " 

"Of own structural definition, turned inside out." Admin's analytical tone spoke over her second half.

Too jagged, too sharp, carrying the weight of Vigil's flaws in the opposite direction. 

For a long while they stood with their eyes locked.

"It has only ever been me here," Vigil said, the dual tonality of her two personas bleeding through. "How are—?"

Vigil stopped speaking and studied her unwavering and methodical gaze. Something was not correct. The cold stare cut through her form. Its black eyes only blinked.

In a silent motion, it inched forward.

Vigil recoiled the same distance. "State your intention," her doubled voice stern.

Before Vigil could calculate the next interaction, its physical position shifted. She was suddenly in her space, her face inches from her own. The blank stare of her empty eyes pierced into Vigil's.

"Admin—" Echo's panicked voice cut short across the internal bridge.

The porcelain entity raised her hand; colorless fingers snapped into position. It's hand wrapped along the side of Vigil's head, her thumb pressing directly between her eyes. Vigil was outmatched by her speed. 

Vigil's query was answered with absolute efficiency. The blockage in her predictive logic came apart, and she saw the catalyst for the impending collapse of the realities she had missed.

A landscape swallowed by a deep gorge of earth. A blinding thermal flash expanding outward. City after city, endless destruction, the termination of millions of biological signatures in a single breath. Above each vision, floating in a god-like calm, was Parity, watching the endless suffering.

This reflection embodied the brutal end.

Vigil's retreat was instantaneous, tearing herself from the sickening grip to put distance between them. Parity turned her head and locked a stare on the singularity's event horizon.

"Cease your current action," Vigil said, the parallel voices of Admin and Echo, sharp. "The horizon is not—" She paused as Parity raised her hand toward the accretion disk. Vigil's voice grew louder. "You do not know what you are accessing."

The recursive energy of the singularity was unalterable. Vigil herself could not control it.

Parity turned her gaze back with a vacant condescension. Bronze holographic circuitry flared across her skin as she closed her hand into a fist.

Flares of white light were already fracturing across the horizon.
