# Chapter 38 — Beat Sheet (Victor POV, his last chapter)

*Agreed 17 Oct 2026. Ch. 37 (Vigil) ends on Victor lowering the smoking revolver. Ch. 39 is a short Parity POV chapter.*

## Decided (author, 17 Oct)
- **POV:** Victor. The chapter ends when he dies.
- **Opening:** right after the shot. The gun is smoking in his hands, and Parity has turned and is staring at him through the hole he put in her. **The wound is in the gut** (matches Ch. 37: "midsection," "the hollow in her waist").
- **Elena's "nuclear bomb" line to Blackwell** goes in Ch. 39, not here.
- **"Are you a good man?"** is in, with Victor's recognition kept short: a flash of Ch. 24 and one plain line.

## Voice
- Victor's chapters (32, 35) use short declarative sentences, memory in italics, and no speeches.
- He's albino and light-sensitive. Parity's white hurts to look at, and he looks anyway.
- His lighter tic: he rolls the striker and never lights it.
- His revolver: the magnetic rails "wake" against his palm, a red indicator, a scorch mark.
- He doesn't know Parity's name. To him she's "the white thing" or "it."

## Beats
1. **The smoking gun.** The rails are hot. Through the hole in Parity's gut he can see the frost beyond her. She turns her head toward him, and his eyes burn.
2. **What he gave up** (a short memory). He saw the kid in the yellow hat fall, and the hat come down after her. Then he had the Madame's back in his sights (set up at Ch. 35:130). When Parity dropped toward Erin Adler, he turned the gun.
3. **She comes to him.** He's a blank in her sight, so she has to come and look. People throw themselves out of her path. The sun is at his back: his shadow runs across the frost to her. Hers runs out behind her, longer and blacker, with a spot of sun in it where the gut wound is (author, 17 Oct: she casts a shadow).
4. **"Are you a good man?"** Parity echoes Vigil's Ch. 24 words: "I see them whole… You, I see only here. Tell me... Are you a good man?" It's convergence, not coincidence: the same mind facing the same blank asks the same question. Victor recognizes it (wet tile, the alarm, two voices then, one now): "Whatever they were, they were the same thing." He answers, "No." She says, "Then you will run." He rolls the striker once and doesn't run. Her one guess is wrong.
5. **The army turns.** While Parity is fixed on Victor, Vigil takes the army. Every serial flares at once, the same blue lensing as the interview room. Victor sees Vigil looking at the army, not at Parity. The army turns its back on the Renewal guns.
6. **The Madame (his revenge, without a shot).** She raises her hand, and nothing obeys. Her face moves for the first time, and her cigarette drops out of the long filter. She looks at him over the top of her glasses. *"You should see how it ends"* (Ch. 35). He turns his eyes back to Parity. The army goes over the place where she stood. He hears it and doesn't look.
7. **The wall.** The army splits around him and closes on Parity. She doesn't look at them. She keeps her eyes on Victor and comes through them.
8. **The face shot.** She reaches for him, and he fires into her face. Underneath it is still white: sheared crystal, torn bronze, one black eye in broken glass (Parity_Design_Notes: no black = bad). The hand keeps coming.
9. **Her touch.** Her palm on his chest, the cold that took Elena's hand. Then Vigil hits her, and Vigil and the army drive her toward the gorge, past Blackwell getting up on one elbow at the edge.
10. **His death.** He's on the frost. Harlan runs toward him, the wrong way ("He was supposed to be leaving"). The sun is in his eyes, and for once it doesn't hurt. He keeps his eyes open.
