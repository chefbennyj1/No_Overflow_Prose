# NO OVERFLOW - Climax Roadmap & Key Scenes

This document tracks the massive narrative convergence and the final set-piece battles for the climax of the book. 

## 1. Parity's Escape (The Speed-Duplication Scene)
*   **The Setup:** This scene is the catalyst that launches the climax. Parity breaks containment.
*   **The Mechanic:** Parity moves through physical space so rapidly that she creates optical afterimages, reducing Blackwell to primal panic as the "afterimages" close in on him.

## 2. The Convergence (The Plaza)
*   **The Threat:** The black hole at the center of the Mnemosyne Engine grows out of control. The gravitational distortion breaches the surface, threatening to consume the city blocks above it. 
*   **The Payoff:** Every major character converges on the Takeda-Holden Plaza, delivering on the ensemble collision teased at the end of Act 1 without forcing them underground.

## 3. Building the Spectacle (Physical Reaction)
*   The duel between Parity and Vigil is not described using "gods in flight." It is described purely through physical, grounded objects reacting to the anomaly:
    *   Rain that stops falling and hangs in the air over the plaza.
    *   Every clock in the city disagreeing (paying off Harper's watch drift from Ch. 27).
    *   The doubled edges spreading from the tower to the whole skyline, making the city look like a badly registered print.
    *   Frost climbing eighty stories up the Takeda-Holden building in the middle of a riot.
    *   Brass casings from a Renewal volley floating exactly where they fell.

## 4. The Fight Stops the War
*   The strongest image: the uprising and the Renewal line both stop shooting and look up. Weapons lowered, barricades half-built. The class war becomes instantly irrelevant.
*   This proves Parity's thesis in one wide shot: she isn't fighting humanity. They are just weather to her.

## 5. The "Clincher" & The Dual Sacrifice
*   **The Tackle:** Vigil has to physically tackle Parity back into the engine—the one domain where the math still binds them together. 
*   **Blackwell's Role:** Blackwell must sacrifice himself to flip the bit at the boundary, opening the way and aiming it so Vigil can carry Parity through. Neither sacrifice is redundant. 

## 6. What "Sealed" Means (The Boundary Paradox)
*   **The Lore:** The shadow (Parity) exists only to balance the enforced order (Vigil). 
*   **The Bleak Victory:** You must remove both halves. There can be nothing left to balance. The engine goes inert. It stops being a universe and goes back to being a machine with a dead star inside it. 

## 7. PR-21 Broadcasts the End
*   The Company's cameras are pointed at the Plaza, broadcasting the end of the world to every screen in the Dead Circuits. 
*   **The Bell at the End of the Motif:** PR-21, the synthetic anchor whose face never changes, breaks. She is the one thing in New Veridia that has never reacted, and she finally reacts.

## 8. The Freeze as a Public Event (POV Structure)
*   The structure is: public spectacle → the world stops on one frozen image → the private resolution inside that instant → the consequence, public again. Everybody sees the beginning and the end. Nobody sees what decided it.
*   **The Ground POV (Victor):** The public half belongs to Victor in the Plaza. This is where the Madame beat lives—Victor sees her face react in the one instant nobody else is looking. 
*   **The Frozen POV (Vigil):** The frozen half belongs to Vigil, the only one who can perceive inside the time dilation. 
*   **The Guardrail:** A clean break between the POVs. No head-hopping. One describing word per situation. No narrating awe. Rain that won't fall does more than any adjective.
