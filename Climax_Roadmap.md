# NO OVERFLOW - Climax Roadmap & Key Scenes

This document tracks the massive narrative convergence and the final set-piece battles for the climax of the book. 

## 1. Parity's Escape (The Speed-Duplication Scene)
*   **The Setup:** This scene is the catalyst that launches the climax. Parity breaks containment.
*   **The Mechanic:** Parity moves through physical space so rapidly that she creates optical afterimages. To Silas Blackwell, it appears as though she is duplicating herself. She exists in two places in his persistence of vision, stripping the genius of his control and reducing him to primal panic as the "afterimages" close in on him from multiple angles.
*   **The Result:** Parity gets the best of Blackwell and escapes, destabilizing the engine.

## 2. The Convergence & The Engine's Collapse
*   **The Threat:** The black hole (the physical/digital void) at the center of the Mnemosyne Engine begins growing out of control. Its event horizon threatens to consume everything. 
*   **The Payoff:** Every major character (Rin, Victor, Vigil, Parity, Blackwell, Iris, Elena) is pulled into this single location. This delivers on the massive ensemble collision that was teased at the end of Act 1.

## 3. The Final Fight (Vigil vs. Parity)
*   **The Time-Stop Mechanic:** As the engine's event horizon reaches critical mass, time slows down to a near-absolute stop. 
*   **Vigil's Evolution:** In this suspended time, Vigil physically blasts out of her synthetic body, transcending her hardware to fight Parity in her pure, unbound form.
*   **The Stakes (Static Suspension):** The entire battle takes place while the rest of the room is frozen in a horrific tableau:
    *   Rin is frozen a microsecond away from death.
    *   Victor is frozen mid-sacrifice to save them.
    *   The event horizon is frozen right before it consumes the lab.

## 4. The "Clincher"
*   While fighting Parity in this suspended time, Vigil notices something crucial that Parity is blind to. 
*   Blackwell—frozen in the room—is holding or possessing a "clincher." It is the one mechanical, digital, or physical key that can stop the black hole and save everyone. 
*   *Note: The exact nature of this item is TBD, but it bridges Blackwell's genius with Vigil's power to defeat Parity.*
