# Chapter 37 — Beat Sheet (Vigil POV)

*Proposed 13 Oct 2026. Not yet confirmed. Ch. 36 is Rin's, ending with the ceiling coming down. Ch. 38 is Victor's last chapter.*

## Voice (from Ch. 5 and 9)
- More poetic than the other POVs. House rules still apply (banned words, plain prose, few similes).
- Admin and Echo talk across the internal bridge. Admin calculates, and Echo intuits.
- She sees futures as threads, branches and world lines ending.
- Parity is "not in the simulation." Victor and Blackwell (the Ambiguities) are blanks to her.
- Climax_Roadmap §3: show the duel through physical objects reacting, never "gods in flight."

## Beats

**1. Inside the instant.** Open where Ch. 36 ends, from inside her perception. The ceiling hangs in the air, the dust stands still, and Harper's "Erin—" is held unfinished. For the first time since the engine, Admin and Echo speak across the bridge. Her sight comes back whole after the RA body narrowed it. The first thing she sees is every branch for the four of them ending under the slab.

**2. The rescue in frozen time.** She carries them out one at a time, up the atrium, through what Rin walked through in Ch. 36, now standing still: the volley's brass hanging in the shaft, and the fallen synth wearing her face, which she passes without stopping. Each trip costs her, because the horizon pulls at her harder every time.

**3. The plaza.** She sets all four down together in the sun, Iris and Elena included, and time starts again. Under the plaza, the ground is sagging into a gorge. She recognizes it from Ch. 9, the vision Parity forced on her: *"A landscape swallowed by a deep gorge of earth."* That future is now arriving.

**4. The war.** Renewal and the army are fighting on the stones. In her sight the frames have no futures, but she can feel them, like limbs she hasn't moved yet. Echo wants to reach for them, and Admin says not yet. This plants the takeover without spending it.

**5. The apex.** She rises. Blackwell is a blank in Parity's hand. Their exchange from Remaining_Outline:
- Vigil: *"What are you?"*
- Parity: *"What you are not."*

**6. The sky fight is fought over futures.** Each exchange is the two of them pulling branches toward or away from their ends, and every branch is lives below. On the plaza, the reader sees it through objects: snow stops falling, clocks disagree, frost climbs the tower. Vigil is losing, because Parity can read her and she can't read Parity. A protector is predictable: "It is the way she is built" (Ch. 33).

**7. The bait.** Parity drops toward Rin, Harper and Harlan. *(Proposed:)* She lets Blackwell go on the way down, near the edge of the gorge. The experiment was inconclusive, so he's no use to her now. Vigil sees him fall out of her sight, the same as he was never in it. She watches all of Rin's, Harper's and Harlan's branches end and can't reach them in time.

**8. The end.** A hole opens in Parity from nowhere. No branch showed it, because it's Victor, an Ambiguity, a blank in Vigil's sight too. Last line: for the first time, Vigil doesn't know what happens next. Ch. 38 picks up on the ground.

1. **Getting Blackwell to the boundary:** he's held at the apex, and the horizon is now under the plaza, not in the ruined lab. Proposed: Parity lets him go on her way down to the bait (beat 7). The boundary can then be the edge of the gorge.
2. **How Blackwell dies:** Remaining_Outline has Parity kill him with the necrotic touch that took Elena's hand. Climax_Roadmap has the boundary conditions kill him. Pick one, or let her touch reach him as he flips it.
3. **Iris's secret:** with Iris on the plaza, who hears her say "something has to go back in"? Blackwell, as the cue for the flip, or Elena?
4. **Elena and Blackwell:** with both on the plaza, there's room for her reserved line: *"You intentionally armed a nuclear bomb just so you could admire the clockwork inside it."* In 37 or 38?
5. **What the rescue costs Vigil:** her form thinning, or the horizon dragging her. A visible toll makes it believable that she can lose the sky fight.
