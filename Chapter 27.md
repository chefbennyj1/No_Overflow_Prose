The streets of Oshima had fractured. The usual flow of pedestrians in the intersections gone. The streets were empty, replaced by an unsettled energy. Down the block, the sound of metal shattering glass. The falling glass echoed sharply over the rain. The people of the Dead Circuits were targeting Takeda-Holden supply stores. Shadows slipped through the jagged frames of the Company windows, pulling out armfuls of stolen supplies and running into the dusk.

Rin kept her head down, moving quickly through the flooded intersection. The top half of her orange lockup jumpsuit was peeled down and tied tightly around her waist. She wore a damp white cotton tank top. The intricate, dark ink of her tattoos exposed on her wet skin.

She was following Vigil.

Seeing the entity move through the physical world in an unarmored synthetic Renewal tactical agents body, was deeply unnerving. Vigil moved with a hyper-optimized precision. Her gait was perfect, lacking the natural sway of a human. She processed the cracked pavement and the flooded streets with calculated, kinetic efficiency.

A group of civilians rushed past Rin and Vigil on the pavement. Vigil stepped sideways, but their shoulders colliding with hers. They were sprinting hard, their faces desperately checking over their shoulders. The heavy, mechanical thud of synchronized boots echoed from the adjacent block. She knew exactly why they were running and what was marching up the street.
One of the looters looked at Rin, then looked at Vigil. His eyes grew wide, all color ran from his face. He stared at the  `RA` serial number glowing under Vigil's eye. He fell into Rin, pushing on her before getting his barring and running fast.


"She moves like one of them," a familiar voice called out over the rain.

Rin stopped. Harper was standing in the mouth of a narrow alleyway, her eyes locked on her. 

A wave of relief crashed into Rin's chest. She ran toward Harper, wrapping her arms around her in a tight, desperate hug. It had been four weeks of hell. 

"I'm so glad to see you," Rin breathed into Harper's jacket. 

Harper hugged her back, her grip firm. When they pulled apart, Harper looked past her, out at the chaotic street. The sharp crack of a rifle echoed in the distance. 

"Something is going on out here," Harper said, her voice tight. "The pots boiling over."
